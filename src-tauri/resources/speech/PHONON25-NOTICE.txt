NOTICE for the Phonon-2.5 GGUF files in this repository
=======================================================

The files phonon-2.5-*.gguf and phonon-2.5-english-*.gguf are converted copies of
the Phonon-2.5 and Phonon-2.5-English models by Fermion Research
(https://huggingface.co/FermionResearch/Phonon-2.5 and
https://huggingface.co/FermionResearch/Phonon-2.5-English), which are derived from
NVIDIA's parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3 (https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3).
The weights are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
licence (CC-BY-4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ (text in
licenses/CC-BY-4.0.txt).

Changes made here: the weights were converted to GGUF with parakeet.cpp
(https://github.com/mudler/parakeet.cpp), expanded to ordinary tensors and, for the
F16, Q8_0 and Q4_K files, stored in that precision. The packed files keep the
five-value linear layers as Fermion ships them. Nothing was trained or fine-tuned
here.

The upstream notices follow, unchanged.
